# Voice & Terminology

## Tone

Hushed. Sparse. The app barely speaks. When it does, lowercase,
no punctuation unless necessary. The words should feel like a whisper
in a quiet room.

- "a topic..." not "Enter a topic here"
- "writing..." not "Generating your haiku..."
- "no haikus yet" not "No haikus yet. Try a topic above!"
- "remove" not "Delete"

## Error Messages

Soft: "something went wrong" — never shouty, never technical.
